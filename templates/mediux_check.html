Check your Mediux files

Select one or more files (mediux-series.yml, mediux-animes.yml…): the app reads them, compares them to your TV show libraries on Plex, and lists, for each file, (1) what's listed but doesn't exist in your library (the "Season: X not found" / "Episode: Y in Season: Z … not found" errors from Kometa, along with the show name) and (2) the shows in the file that are missing from Plex. Finally, (3) the Plex shows that none of the files cover.

Ctrl (or Cmd) + click to select more than one at a time.
Analysis in progress: the app is reading the episodes of each show in the files from Plex; this may take up to one minute the first time. The other pages remain available during this time.
